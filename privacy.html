Privacy policy

Steam Deck Screenshots · Updated 19 October 2026

What the tool uses

This personal tool reads Steam screenshot images, their filenames, game names and Steam account folder identifiers from the owner's Deck. It copies those images to the owner's Google Drive, organized by game and Steam account folder.

The tool requests Google's drive.file permission. This permits access to the specific files created by the app or explicitly opened with it, rather than general access to the account's Drive contents. It reads backup file metadata and checksums to check uploads.

Storage and sharing

Google authorization tokens are stored in a private configuration file on the Deck. Pending image copies are kept on the Deck until their upload succeeds. Upload records and logs are kept on the Deck. Original screenshots remain in Steam's screenshot folders.

Screenshot images and backup folder metadata are sent directly to Google Drive. This tool has no separate upload server, advertising service or analytics service, and does not sell or share Google user data with other parties. Using this information site does not give it access to the owner's screenshots or Google tokens.

This public information site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web request information under its privacy statement. Google processes Google account and Drive data under its privacy policy.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the uploader on the Deck and revoke its Google access through Google Account permissions. The owner can delete backup files in Drive and remove local queue, upload records, logs and configuration files from the Deck. Stopping uploads does not automatically delete existing backups or original screenshots.

Steam Deck Screenshots' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this personal project, contact the project owner on GitHub.
